## 📌 Overview

The objective of the hackathon was to design and backtest an AI/ML-driven quantitative investment strategy that rebalances monthly across a global equity universe.

* **Universe & Coverage:** Large-scale historical equities from 28 countries (2005 – June 2025, available at [`stocks.csv`](./python_files/data/stocks.csv)).
* **Out-of-Sample (OOS) Testing Window:** January 2015 – May 2025.
* **Benchmark:** S&P 500.
* **Evaluation Pillars:**
  1. **Economic Intuition:** Rationale behind factor selection, signal engineering, and alpha generation.
  2. **Machine Learning Architecture:** Soundness of ML/AI models, cross-validation discipline, and prevention of lookahead bias.
  3. **Risk-Adjusted Performance:** Realized out-of-sample portfolio metrics (Sharpe Ratio, Annualized Alpha, Information Ratio, Drawdown control).
